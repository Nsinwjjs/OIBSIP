# OIBSIP Data Analytics – Level 1 Task 1
## Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

### 📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte (OIBSIP) Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** and identify useful patterns related to sales, customers, products, and purchasing behavior.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

- Understand the retail sales dataset
- Analyze sales performance across product categories
- Identify sales trends over time
- Analyze customer demographics
- Study customer behavior by age group and gender
- Identify top-selling products
- Analyze revenue contribution by product category
- Explore the relationship between price and quantity
- Identify relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis
- Present insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- Retail Sales Dataset
- GitHub

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains retail transaction-level information including fields such as:

- Transaction ID
- Customer ID
- Date
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Maximum Sales

The dataset was cleaned and analyzed in Power BI before creating the dashboard.

# 📈 Dashboard Structure

## Page 1 — Sales Overview

This page focuses on the overall sales performance of the business.

### Visualizations
- Total Sales by Product Category
- Average Sales by Product Category
- Sales Trend Over Time
- Quantity Sold by Product Category
- KPI cards for key business metrics

### Key Observation
The page provides an overview of sales performance and helps compare the contribution of different product categories and time periods.

---

## 👥 Page 2 — Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer demographics and purchasing behavior.

### Visualizations
- Customer Distribution by Gender
- Customers by Age Group
- Total Sales Amount by Age Group
- Average Sales by Gender
- Quantity Purchased by Gender

### Age Groups
Customers were grouped into:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 56+

### Key Observation
Customer segmentation by age and gender helps identify important customer groups and supports targeted marketing decisions.

---

## 🛍️ Page 3 — Product & Business Insights

This page focuses on product performance and deeper business insights.

### Visualizations
- Top 10 Best-Selling Products
- Revenue by Product Category
- Price vs Quantity Relationship
- Correlation Heatmap
- Business KPI cards

### Key Observation
The product analysis helps identify high-performing products, revenue-generating categories, and relationships between price and purchasing quantity.

# 🔎 Additional Analysis

## Price vs Quantity

A scatter visualization was used to study the relationship between product price and quantity purchased.

This analysis can help identify:

- High-price products with high or low demand
- Low-price products with high purchase volume
- Unusual purchasing patterns
- Differences between product categories

---

## Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was included to examine relationships between numerical variables such as:

- Age
- Price per Unit
- Quantity
- Total Amount
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Maximum Sales

Correlation values help identify variables that have stronger positive or negative relationships.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard provides several useful business insights:

1. Product categories can be compared based on their total revenue and sales volume.
2. Customer age groups provide useful segmentation opportunities.
3. Gender-based analysis helps understand differences in purchasing behavior.
4. Top-selling products can be prioritized for inventory and promotional planning.
5. Price and quantity analysis can reveal useful demand patterns.
6. Correlation analysis helps identify relationships between numerical business variables.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Categories
Businesses should prioritize inventory, promotions, and marketing campaigns for product categories that generate higher revenue.

### 2. Use Customer Segmentation
Age and gender analysis can be used to create more targeted marketing campaigns and personalized offers.

### 3. Optimize Inventory
Top-selling products should receive appropriate inventory priority to reduce the risk of stock-outs.

### 4. Review Pricing Strategy
The relationship between price and quantity can support better pricing and promotional decisions.

### 5. Monitor Sales Trends
Sales trends should be reviewed regularly to identify periods of high and low demand and improve sales planning.

The Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights.
